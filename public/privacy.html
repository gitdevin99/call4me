Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Can You Call helps you ask an AI assistant to find and call businesses on your behalf. This page explains the information used to provide that service.

Information we use

When you sign in with Google, we receive your account identifier, name, email address, and profile image if available. We use this to create and secure your account. We also process the requests, messages, voice notes, business choices, and call instructions you provide. If you add credit or place a call, we keep payment transaction, balance, call status, duration, cost, result, and transcript records needed to operate the service and resolve issues.

Voice notes and calls

Voice notes are recorded only with your microphone permission. The app keeps the voice note audio in storage on your device and sends it for transcription when you submit it. For live calls, our AI assistant tells the business that it is an AI assistant and that the conversation is transcribed, and asks whether it may continue. Call transcripts and outcomes are associated with your account. The business hears the information needed to carry out your request.

Service providers

We use Supabase for sign-in and account data, Google for sign-in and business search, OpenAI for AI and transcription, Twilio or another configured telephone carrier for calls, Whop for prepaid credit payments, and Supermemory for persistent assistant memory. Those providers process the information necessary to perform their functions. We do not sell your personal information.

Assistant memory

While memory is enabled, new chat exchanges, details you save, and completed call transcripts and outcomes are sent to Supermemory to retrieve relevant context across conversations. You can inspect your memory, correct details, pause it, or clear it in Profile. Clearing immediately stops use of the old memory and queues its deletion with the provider; your original chat, call, and payment records remain separate. New memories can take time to process.

Product analytics and session replay

We use PostHog to understand app visits, sign-ins, request submissions, checkout opens, and call starts. Signed-in activity is associated with your account identifier; we do not send your email, message contents, phone numbers, or call transcripts as analytics event properties. PostHog also records masked screen interactions to help us diagnose navigation and usability problems. Text and input values are masked, and conversation messages, transcripts, profile cards, call details, and audio/video elements are blocked from these recordings. Request bodies, request headers, and console logs are not recorded. PostHog uses browser storage to associate visits and sessions.

Storage and control

Your conversation draft and voice note audio may be stored on your device so you can resume later. Account, credit, and call records are stored on our servers. You can sign out in the app. For access or deletion requests, contact us at sanjagptai@gmail.com. We may need to retain transaction or call records where required to operate the service, handle disputes, or comply with law.

Security and changes

We restrict account data to the signed-in user and the server operations needed to provide the service. We may update this policy as the product changes and will update the effective date here. For privacy questions, contact sanjagptai@gmail.com.
